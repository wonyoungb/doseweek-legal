1. The service

These terms apply to the DoseWeek apps for iPhone, iPad and Android and to this help website. DoseWeek is a personal record-keeping tool for injection schedules, administrations and related notes, run by Wonyoung Choi, an individual developer based in the Republic of Korea. By using DoseWeek you agree to these terms. The privacy policy for your platform explains how information is handled.

2. Free and Plus

DoseWeek is a free download, and the free version shows ads to Free users. Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription, monthly or annual, that removes ads and adds period summaries and comparisons, a visit report editor, Today layout presets and meal templates.

Features that were in DoseWeek before Plus remain free and are not moved into Plus. If Plus ends, your records, templates and saved settings are kept. The same Free policy applies to everyone: buying an earlier paid version of DoseWeek does not include Plus.

The price and billing period are shown in the app and in the store before you buy.

3. Billing, renewal and cancellation

Plus is sold and billed by the App Store on iPhone and iPad and by Google Play on Android, using the account you are signed in with. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. Cancel in the store's subscription settings, which you can open from Settings in DoseWeek. After you cancel, Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own refund policies. If the price of Plus changes, the store tells you in advance under its own rules and, where those rules require it, asks for your agreement before the new price applies. Plus bought in one store works only in DoseWeek on that platform; there is no DoseWeek account to share it across platforms.

On iPhone and iPad (App Store)

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings. A change between monthly and annual takes effect at your next renewal date. Request refunds from Apple.

Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA) also applies to the iOS app.
https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

On Android (Google Play)

Payment is charged to the payment method in your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions; cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately, and Google Play credits the unused monthly time toward the annual plan; switching from annual to monthly takes effect at your next renewal date. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies, and Google Play's terms also apply to your purchase.

4. Optional ad-free pass

In the free version you can choose to watch one rewarded ad and then use DoseWeek without ads for 30 minutes. The condition is shown before the ad starts. The pass is granted only when the ad reports that the reward was earned; closing the ad early grants nothing. The pass is optional, is not a purchase, has no cash value and cannot be transferred. Declining it changes no Free feature.

5. Avertissement médical

DoseWeek est un outil personnel d’enregistrement et ne fournit pas d’avis médical, de diagnostic ni de traitement. Consultez toujours votre médecin ou votre pharmacien pour toute décision concernant votre traitement, y compris le calendrier et les doses.

Cette application n’est pas un dispositif médical. Elle ne permet pas de déterminer si vous présentez une pathologie et n’est pas destinée à diagnostiquer, traiter ou prévenir une maladie.

Si vous pensez être en situation d’urgence médicale, contactez immédiatement les services d’urgence locaux. N’attendez pas une réponse par e-mail de l’assistance.

6. Your records

Your records stay on your device and are yours. DoseWeek has no account, and the developer does not receive your health records. You are responsible for keeping your device, backups, exports and recovery codes safe; the developer cannot recover lost records, files or codes. Plus does not change where your records are stored.
