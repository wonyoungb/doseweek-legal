DoseWeek · Android 1.0.6

Help for DoseWeek on Android

This text takes effect on October 3, 2026. The parts about accounts, sync, Plus, ads and other new features describe the first DoseWeek version that has them (1.0.6) and apply from the release of that version. Earlier versions do not have these features. You may optionally sign in to a DoseWeek account with Apple or Google. Existing local-record features remain available without sign-in. Linking a verified Plus subscription from either store to the same DoseWeek account enables encrypted sync on both platforms. Verified Plus also shares ad removal and Plus tools across both platforms. To revoke Apple sign-in, the server may retain an Apple refresh token encrypted with a separate server key, distinct from health-record end-to-end keys. Usage analytics and its international transfer each need separate optional consent, independent of account sign-in, sync and ad choices. Refusing either keeps analytics off and core features available. Health records and recovery codes are not sent to analytics.

Getting started

How to use DoseWeek in six steps, from setup to your first record and backup.

  1. Open DoseWeek and set it up

    Confirm that you are 18 or older, read the privacy notice and choose whether to allow optional usage analytics; every core feature works without it. Then set up your plan, or restore a backup, import records from another app or manage records without a plan.

    Optional services and usage analytics →
  2. Set your schedule

    Choose your medication and dose, then pick Repeating schedule or Pick dates; you can change the plan later in Settings > Treatment plan. Reminders are optional.

    Can I choose specific injection dates? →
  3. Record an injection

    On Today, tap Record administration, answer Yes, I did, then check the details and save. Your records appear in History, where you can also add a past injection or correct a record.

    Correct a record →
  4. Record meals

    In the Body tab, open Meals. Enter only the values you know, or search the built-in offline food list.

    Can I record meals and nutrition? →
  5. Import earlier records

    Tap Import records from another app at the top of History or Settings. Review the records from your CSV, TSV or JSON file; only the ones you select are added.

    Can I bring records from another app? →
  6. Backups, reminders and privacy choices

    In Settings, export an encrypted backup, adjust Notifications and change Usage analytics (optional) at any time. Add the DoseWeek widget to your Home Screen to see your next injection at a glance. While Plus is active, you can separately consent to turn on automatic server backup and sync. App records are end-to-end encrypted on the device; the server cannot read the backed-up contents. Free users can still manually export and import files and use the in-app backup reminder. Android OS Google Drive Auto Backup includes DoseWeek app data only while Plus is verified and active; it then backs up the database, keys and records together as one complete set. If Plus is not active or cannot be verified, for example offline or when the store check fails, DoseWeek skips that backup run instead of sending an empty or partial backup, so the previous backup stays as it is. DoseWeek does not delete existing Drive backups, and you can still restore an existing backup. This backup is kept in your Google account under Google's rules, separately from DoseWeek server copies; you can manage or delete it in your Google account's backup settings. Users of 1.0.5 receive a one-time notice explaining this change and how to export a file. The notice does not block use of the app.

    Can I back up or restore my records? →

Do not include health information, medication or administration records, personal notes, a backup file, or a recovery code in a support email.

FAQ and troubleshooting

Where are my records stored?

The developer does not receive them in readable form; if you turn on account sync, they are sent to the server only end-to-end encrypted, so the developer cannot read them. Content you consent to and send to the AI record assistant (Pro) is the exception to end-to-end encryption: the DoseWeek server and Google can read it while an answer is being made. Google can also read it for as long as Google holds it: in memory for up to 24 hours, or for up to 90 days when Google’s abuse checks flag a request, which authorized Google staff may then review.

While Plus is active, you can separately consent to turn on automatic server backup and sync. App records are end-to-end encrypted on the device; the server cannot read the backed-up contents. Free users can still manually export and import files and use the in-app backup reminder.

Do I need to sign in or create an account?

This text takes effect on October 3, 2026. The parts about accounts, sync, Plus, ads and other new features describe the first DoseWeek version that has them (1.0.6) and apply from the release of that version. Earlier versions do not have these features.

You may optionally sign in to a DoseWeek account with Apple or Google. Existing local-record features remain available without sign-in. Linking a verified Plus subscription from either store to the same DoseWeek account enables encrypted sync on both platforms. Verified Plus also shares ad removal and Plus tools across both platforms. To revoke Apple sign-in, the server may retain an Apple refresh token encrypted with a separate server key, distinct from health-record end-to-end keys.

If you turn it on separately, DoseWeek encrypts all your records on the device (injection plans and their changes, dose records, meals, body measurements, supplies and import history) together with supported settings, for automatic backup and sync across both platforms. The settings that sync are appearance (colour and light or dark mode), the weight-change unit, the body map view, whether injection reminders are on, whether reminders show details or the medication name, recording without a plan, supply tracking, and your food search market and search-language choices. When one of these is changed on another device, this device applies it and tells you once about an important change, such as reminders or supply tracking. Sync never turns on an AI feature: you turn it on yourself on each device. Permissions, connections and display choices that belong to a single device are not synced: notification permission, the app lock, health-data, calendar and cloud-storage connections, and widget appearance. When you restore from Settings, the saved settings are applied and the app tells you which settings changed. This is end-to-end encryption: the server keeps only encrypted snapshots and the account and purchase records it needs. Your data key is stored only in a form locked by your recovery code, and the recovery code stays on your devices, so the operator cannot read your health records. The server handles account and provider identifiers, verified Plus state, ciphertext, versions and minimal access metadata, but receives neither readable health records nor your recovery code. A new device needs the recovery code; if it is lost, the developer cannot recover the encrypted data. An empty device may restore after code confirmation; a device with existing records requires a preview and merge confirmation. To recheck subscription status with the store, the server also keeps keyed purchase digests and encrypted lookup references containing an Apple original transaction ID or store purchase token. References use AES-256-GCM with a server-only key separate from the end-to-end health-record keys and are used only for store checks. Account deletion erases references; expired references are pruned after 30 days. Keyed digest markers that prevent duplicate purchase linking are kept for 5 years as records of payment and supply under Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and are then deleted. Google can also read it for as long as Google holds it: in memory for up to 24 hours, or for up to 90 days when Google’s abuse checks flag a request, which authorized Google staff may then review.

Usage analytics and its international transfer each need separate optional consent, independent of account sign-in, sync and ad choices. Refusing either keeps analytics off and core features available. Health records and recovery codes are not sent to analytics.

A public announcement can be checked when the app opens, before onboarding. The per-announcement ‘Do not show for one week’ choice stays on this device. The announcement request may process connection data such as an IP address; the web server keeps an access log of the IP address, time, requested address, response status and size, referrer and browser or app identifier (user agent) for up to 11 days. This request also passes through Cloudflare; the privacy policy gives the overseas transfer details.

Does DoseWeek use AI or Health Connect?

This part applies from the first DoseWeek version that has the AI record assistant (1.0.6). Until that version is released, the app has no AI record assistant and sends nothing to Google for it.

DoseWeek 1.0.6 for Android includes the optional AI record assistant (Pro). It is processed on a server, not on the device. Only after separate consent, generative AI on Google Cloud Vertex AI (Global, no fixed country) creates weekly summaries, general meal ideas and app-help answers. No other feature uses generative AI. Optional on-device voice and food-label recognition help with input; Google food-label SDK diagnostics require separate consent, as described in the Privacy Policy. Health Connect is now an optional read-only import of weight, height, body fat percentage, and lean body mass: the app asks for read permissions only, never write or delete, and imports nothing until you grant that permission. Every administration record is still entered and confirmed by hand.

Does DoseWeek provide notifications, reminders, or widgets?

Optional reminders and a home-screen widget are available. DoseWeek 1.0.6 can schedule local reminders on your device for the next dose, a follow-up after a missed dose, and low supply. They stay off until you turn them on and grant the notification permission. By default the reminder text reveals nothing about your treatment; the medication name appears only if you enable it, and dose amounts never appear. Tapping a reminder only opens the app, so nothing is ever recorded from a notification. There are no wearable apps.

Can I back up or restore my records?

The file-backup details below describe manual export and import. Plus automatic server backup is a separate path.

Yes. In Settings, use Android's system file picker to manually save all app data in an AES-256-GCM encrypted file, and select that file to restore. A separate high-entropy recovery code is required. Backups can be restored only in DoseWeek for Android. DoseWeek on other platforms uses a different backup format, so backups cannot be moved between platforms.

This export needs no DoseWeek account and no broad storage permission, and the export itself uses no network access. If you choose an external or cloud document provider, its terms and policies apply. In either case the developer receives neither the file nor its recovery code and cannot reset a lost code or decrypt the file. Restore completely validates the file before replacing current data in one atomic local transaction.

While Plus is active, you can separately consent to turn on automatic server backup and sync. App records are end-to-end encrypted on the device; the server cannot read the backed-up contents. Free users can still manually export and import files and use the in-app backup reminder.

Android OS Google Drive Auto Backup includes DoseWeek app data only while Plus is verified and active; it then backs up the database, keys and records together as one complete set. How do I delete all records?

Use Delete all data in DoseWeek Settings. Uninstalling the app also causes Android to delete local app data. Backup files saved outside the app are not deleted automatically; delete them separately through Android's file app or their document provider. Backups you uploaded to your own Google Drive are not removed by uninstalling either: delete them from the backup list inside the app, or by removing the app's data from your Google account.

The encrypted server copy is kept while Plus is active. It is deleted 30 days after the end of the last Plus period that the server verified with the App Store or Google Play. Before deleting, the server checks with the store again and keeps the copy if Plus was renewed. If the store cannot be reached, deletion may be postponed by up to 7 days. At any time you can delete only the server copy (reset sync), which keeps your account and the records on your devices, or delete the whole account. If you request direct deletion or withdraw consent, the 30-day waiting period and store-outage extension do not apply. Access stops immediately and the data covered by the request is erased from live storage without delay. Deleting only the server copy keeps the account. Completion is reported after the actual result is confirmed. Server backups are kept for at most 7 days, so deleted data leaves those backups within 7 days. Records on your devices and files you exported or stored separately, for example in a cloud storage service, must be deleted separately. Deleting the DoseWeek account does not cancel a store subscription. DoseWeek account and server-data deletion is reported separately from provider disconnection. If disconnection cannot be confirmed, you receive instructions for manual removal; no automatic retry is promised. After account deletion, the server keeps only a keyed marker of each purchase that was linked to the account, without any link to the account, so that the purchase cannot silently move to another account. This marker is kept for 5 years as a record of payment and supply under Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and is then deleted. Records of consumer complaints and dispute handling, such as support requests, are kept for 3 years under the same article. If you bought Plus on Google Play, the separate purchase-verification server keeps its purchase records (a one-way hash of the purchase token, the product, subscription state and times) until 90 days after the latest of the paid-period end, grace-period end and last check, and notification identifiers for 30 days; these records are not linked to a DoseWeek account and are deleted on that schedule. These purchase-verification records hold only the verification state, with no price, payment method or order details, so they are not payment records. Payment records are kept by the store that sold the subscription.

Email wonyoung@wonyoungchoi.dev to request deletion of your DoseWeek account and associated server data without reinstalling the app or subscribing to Plus. Identify the sign-in provider and, if known, your DoseWeek account ID. We explain identity verification by email. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, health records or purchase tokens. Composing or sending the email does not confirm deletion. Account/server-data erasure and provider disconnection outcomes are confirmed and communicated separately. If disconnection cannot be confirmed, we explain manual removal in the provider’s account settings. Canceling a subscription is not a condition of this request; manage store billing separately.

Choose dates and times

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

Tap a date or time to open the system picker. Use the month or year control for older dates, and AM/PM when shown. Cancel keeps your entry; confirming changes the draft. Save the record to keep your changes. Use the pencil to type dates as YYYY-MM-DD or YYYYMMDD and 24-hour times as HH:mm or HHmm.

Correct a record

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

In History, choose the date, then Edit on the record. Save to update that same record. If you change its date, the updated record is under the new date. Imported Health Connect measurements are read-only: correct them in the original app.

Add a past injection

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

In History, choose a past date and add a past administration. Confirm the medicine, dose, unit, and actual time before saving. You can manage records without creating a plan. Records without a plan do not change schedules or stock. For records linked to a plan, check whether stock will be deducted. Restoring an encrypted backup replaces data; it is a separate action. Stock deduction for past entries starts off; review the displayed choice. If a linked plan’s latest injection changes, future unresolved dates may be adjusted.

Find missing measurements

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

First check the original app and Health Connect. In DoseWeek’s Body screen, check the metric and period. In Settings, open Health Connect and choose Import now. For missing results, check read permissions and permission for past records separately from syncing in the original app. The measurement date is when it was measured, not when DoseWeek imported it.

Understand left and right

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

Left and right always mean your own body. My body and Front view change only how the diagram is displayed. Your selection and saved records keep the same body side. Check the selected name or open the site list to choose by text. You can change the view in Settings too.

Can I record meals and nutrition?

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

In the Body tab, the Meals section takes what you type. Enter the food name and the totals you know (energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat) and leave anything you do not know blank; a blank value stays unknown. A meal holds up to 100 items and you can keep up to 1,000 favorites. You can also search the built-in food list offline. You can now search for 200 foods in the list using names in 17 languages. Search starts with all supported languages; choose specific languages in Settings if you prefer. Records stay on the device and travel in the encrypted backup. Meal records are not nutrition advice or a prescription.

Can I choose specific injection dates?

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

In the schedule editor, choose the chosen-dates mode and pick several dates in the calendar. The selection is shown as a preview, and a future date closer than the minimum interval in the verified medicine information is blocked at save. Records you already made in the past are never blocked. After the last chosen date the plan stays active and asks you to add more dates.

What do the reference ranges on charts mean?

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

On the weekly weight-change and BMI charts you can show a published general reference (WHO, CDC, NHS, NICE, the Korean Society for the Study of Obesity, the Japan Society for the Study of Obesity) or a personal target you set. Nothing is preselected, and the label shows only the range and its source, never a category word. A reference is general population information, not advice about your treatment, and your clinician may set a different target.

How does calendar sync work?

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

It stays off until you turn it on in Settings, and asks for calendar permission separately at that point. Once on, it writes up to 24 upcoming injection days within the next 84 days as 30-minute events in the one calendar you choose. The title is “Personal reminder” by default and becomes “Injection day” only if you choose it; medication, dose, site, notes and alerts are never written. The app updates or deletes only the events it created, and leaves alone any event you edited yourself. Turning sync off lets you delete upcoming events, and events left in the calendar stay there after you uninstall the app.

Can an older version restore a new backup?

This applies to DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

No. The new features raise the backup format number, so an earlier version refuses a file written by the new one. The opposite direction still works: backups written by earlier versions restore in the new version. Meal records and chart reference settings are included in the backup; calendar event links and unsaved drafts are not. Your choice of reference medication for the estimate is not included in a backup; choose it again after a restore.

Which devices and Android versions are supported?

This applies to DoseWeek Android versionCode 13 and later.

Use DoseWeek on Android 14 or later. On Android 8.0 to Android 13, you can keep using the version already installed (versionCode 12), but it will not receive updates, and Google Play will not offer new installs on those devices. Some optional AI features need a compatible device; you can still enter records yourself if they are unavailable.

Can I bring records from another app?

Available in DoseWeek Android 1.0.0 (versionCode 12) and later.

You can import past administrations from a CSV, TSV or JSON file, or paste a table directly. One import takes up to 10,000 rows and the saved review draft is limited to 1 MiB. Symptom and unclassified rows cannot be saved yet and must be deselected. Rows that may be duplicates are marked for review, and nothing is saved until you confirm. This is not a backup restore; the preparation guide is at doseweek-legal.wonyoungchoi.dev/import/.

Is DoseWeek free?

Yes. DoseWeek is a free download on Google Play, and Free users see ads. Recording, History, Body charts, the fixed 4, 8 and 12-week visit summary, meals, manual file exports and imports, backup reminders, calendar sync, reminders, the widget and the app lock remain free.

Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription, monthly or annual, that removes ads and adds extra tools. The price and billing period are shown in the app and on Google Play before you buy.

What does Plus add, and what stays free?

Plus removes ads and adds four tools: period summaries that compare a week or month with the one before; a visit summary editor for the period (up to 26 weeks or a custom range of up to 365 days), sections, order, layout, paper size and whether the records table is included; Today layout presets for the order and visibility of Today's extra cards; and meal templates that open as an unsaved draft for you to review. The tools use only values you recorded and give no medical advice.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Plus renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, which you can open from Settings in DoseWeek. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge; Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately, and Google Play credits the unused monthly time toward the annual plan; switching from annual to monthly takes effect at your next renewal date. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies. Uninstalling the app or deleting its data does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore Plus on a new device or after reinstalling?

Sign in to Google Play with the account you used to subscribe, open DoseWeek and tap Restore purchases in Settings. The app also checks Google Play when it starts and when you return to it, so a purchase made on another device or in Google Play appears the same way.

You may optionally sign in to a DoseWeek account with Apple or Google. Existing local-record features remain available without sign-in. Linking a verified Plus subscription from either store to the same DoseWeek account enables encrypted sync on both platforms. Verified Plus also shares ad removal and Plus tools across both platforms. To revoke Apple sign-in, the server may retain an Apple refresh token encrypted with a separate server key, distinct from health-record end-to-end keys.

If you turn it on separately, DoseWeek encrypts all your records on the device (injection plans and their changes, dose records, meals, body measurements, supplies and import history) together with supported settings, for automatic backup and sync across both platforms. The settings that sync are appearance (colour and light or dark mode), the weight-change unit, the body map view, whether injection reminders are on, whether reminders show details or the medication name, recording without a plan, supply tracking, and your food search market and search-language choices. When one of these is changed on another device, this device applies it and tells you once about an important change, such as reminders or supply tracking. Sync never turns on an AI feature: you turn it on yourself on each device. Permissions, connections and display choices that belong to a single device are not synced: notification permission, the app lock, health-data, calendar and cloud-storage connections, and widget appearance. When you restore from Settings, the saved settings are applied and the app tells you which settings changed. This is end-to-end encryption: the server keeps only encrypted snapshots and the account and purchase records it needs. Your data key is stored only in a form locked by your recovery code, and the recovery code stays on your devices, so the operator cannot read your health records. The server handles account and provider identifiers, verified Plus state, ciphertext, versions and minimal access metadata, but receives neither readable health records nor your recovery code. A new device needs the recovery code; if it is lost, the developer cannot recover the encrypted data. An empty device may restore after code confirmation; a device with existing records requires a preview and merge confirmation. To recheck subscription status with the store, the server also keeps keyed purchase digests and encrypted lookup references containing an Apple original transaction ID or store purchase token. References use AES-256-GCM with a server-only key separate from the end-to-end health-record keys and are used only for store checks. Account deletion erases references; expired references are pruned after 30 days. Keyed digest markers that prevent duplicate purchase linking are kept for 5 years as records of payment and supply under Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and are then deleted. Content you consent to and send to the AI record assistant (Pro) is the exception to end-to-end encryption: the DoseWeek server and Google can read it while an answer is being made. Google can also read it for as long as Google holds it: in memory for up to 24 hours, or for up to 90 days when Google’s abuse checks flag a request, which authorized Google staff may then review.

Why do I see ads, and how do I change my ad choices?

Ads keep the free version free. Free users see a banner on History and in the Measurements section of Body, and occasionally a full-screen ad after closing certain help or food-settings sheets; ads never appear on Today, in the record flow or on safety notices. Ads are non-personalized, and Plus removes them. You can also choose to watch one ad to use DoseWeek without ads for 24 hours; it is optional and not a purchase, and closing the ad early grants nothing. You can earn at most two rewards in any rolling 24 hours, and remaining ad-free time never exceeds 48 hours. While a pass is active, another rewarded ad is offered only when 24 hours or less remain. The pass pauses if the device clock is set back more than five minutes from the latest time the app has seen. While the pass is active, automatic ads and Plus subscription offers are hidden.

Open Settings > Privacy choices for ads to review or change Google's ad consent where your region requires it; elsewhere it explains that no choice is needed. Ad choices are separate from Usage analytics (optional) and from the 18+ confirmation. If ad processing is not allowed, the app shows no ads and every Free feature keeps working. To report an inappropriate ad, tap the AdChoices icon on the ad or use Report an ad in Settings; do not add health details.

I bought DoseWeek when it was a paid app. What changes for me?

New and prior users keep existing core features Free. No separate prior-buyer Plus grant is offered; the prior-buyer claim/code program is retired. An earlier purchase alone starts neither Plus nor a charge. Only the standard free trial applies, for first-time subscribers whose eligibility the store confirms and under the displayed conditions. Applicable statutory rights remain protected. Perpetual ad-free use is not guaranteed. Platform and consumer-law review of historical ad-free rights remains pending.

Recording, History, Body charts, the fixed 4, 8 and 12-week visit summary, meals, manual file exports and imports, backup reminders, calendar sync, reminders, the widget and the app lock remain free. A one-time notice in the app explains the change, and you can read it again in Settings > About > What's new.

Android OS Google Drive Auto Backup includes DoseWeek app data only while Plus is verified and active; it then backs up the database, keys and records together as one complete set. If Plus is not active or cannot be verified, for example offline or when the store check fails, DoseWeek skips that backup run instead of sending an empty or partial backup, so the previous backup stays as it is. DoseWeek does not delete existing Drive backups, and you can still restore an existing backup. This backup is kept in your Google account under Google's rules, separately from DoseWeek server copies; you can manage or delete it in your Google account's backup settings. Users of 1.0.5 receive a one-time notice explaining this change and how to export a file. The notice does not block use of the app.
