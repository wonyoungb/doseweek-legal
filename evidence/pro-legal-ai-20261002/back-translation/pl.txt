lead = On the basis of your entries it creates weekly summaries and general meal ideas and explains how to use the app. The answers are created by AI, not a human, and it does not give medical advice.
sent = Before entries are sent, we show their content and nothing is sent until you touch "Send". We send the number of doses (on time, with delay, skipped), the change in the body mass entered by yourself, the number of records of each kind of symptom, the number of days with a recorded meal together with the protein average and goal, and your streak in weeks. With meal ideas we also send the chosen meal type, preferences, allergies and products you do not like. The language and region setting and the questions you type are also sent, and help about the app adds only the app version, the platform and the current screen.
notSent = We do not send names or doses of medicines, notes, names of food products, photos, dates, nor your first and last name, e-mail address or account data. What you type in the question field is sent exactly as it was written.
where = The app encrypts the content once more and sends it to the DoseWeek server (Seoul, Republic of Korea), and it is processed by Amazon Bedrock from Amazon Web Services (Seoul region). The AI model is Claude of the company Anthropic. AWS operates it, and Anthropic does not see the content.
retention = What you send and the answers we do not leave on the server nor use for training AI. The answers are saved only on your device. Usage counters (without content) we keep up to 2 months, and consent confirmations as long as your account exists. If you report an answer and choose "Attach the content of the answer", only that answer is kept for 30 days.
e2ee = When the AI creates an answer, the DoseWeek server and Amazon Bedrock can read what was sent. Sync and backup data remain protected by end-to-end encryption, as until now.
optional = Only the AI functions will be unavailable; the other functions of Pro and Plus still work. You can withdraw consent at any moment in Settings > AI entries assistant.

checkAge = I am at least 18 years old
processing = The app encrypts the content, Amazon Bedrock (Seoul) processes it, and after the answer is created it is deleted at once.
