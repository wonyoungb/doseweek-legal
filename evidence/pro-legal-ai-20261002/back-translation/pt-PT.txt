lead = It uses your records to create weekly summaries and general meal ideas, and explains how to use the application. The answers are given by an AI, not by a person, and it does not provide medical counselling/advice.
sent = Before sending records, we show the content, and nothing is sent until you touch "Send". We send the number of doses (on time, with delay, omitted), the change in the weight you entered, how many times you recorded each type of symptom, the number of days with recorded meals, together with the protein average and goal, and your sequence in weeks. For meal ideas we also send the meal type, the preferences, the allergies and the foods you do not like, as you chose them. The language and region setting and the questions you write are also sent, and help about the application adds only the application version, the platform and the current screen.
notSent = We do not send names or doses of medicines, notes, food names, photographs, dates, nor your name, e-mail or account data. What you write in the question field is sent as you wrote it.
where = The application encrypts the content one more time and sends it to the DoseWeek server (Seoul, Republic of Korea), and Amazon Bedrock from Amazon Web Services (Seoul region) processes it. The AI model is Claude, from Anthropic. It is operated by AWS, and Anthropic cannot see the content.
retention = What you send and the answers do not stay on the server nor are they used to train AI. The answers are saved only on your device. Usage counts (without content) are kept up to 2 months, and consent records while your account exists. If you report an answer and choose "Include the text of the answer", only that answer is kept for 30 days.
e2ee = While the AI creates an answer, the DoseWeek server and Amazon Bedrock can read what you sent. Sync and backup-copy data continue protected by end-to-end encryption, as now.
optional = Only the AI features become unavailable; the other features of Pro and Plus continue available. You can withdraw consent at any moment in Settings > AI records assistant.
checkHealth = [Optional] I accept the processing of my health information (sensitive)

checkAge = I am 18 years old or more
processing = The application encrypts the content, Amazon Bedrock (Seoul) processes it and it is deleted as soon as the answer is created.
r.dose = I cannot define your dose nor when to administer it. It depends on the prescription and on your body's reaction, so talk to the health professional who prescribed it. I can, instead, summarize your dose history. Do you want a summary to show at the next appointment?
r.sideEffect = I am not able to assess whether a symptom is normal nor whether it is due to the medicine. If it persists or worries you, talk to your health professional. I can summarize the symptoms you recorded.
r.diagnosis = Only a doctor can say whether you have a disease or what the probability of coming to have one is. Talk about your doubts with your health professional. I can help prepare questions for the appointment.
r.drugInfo = I cannot say whether you can combine other medicines or supplements, nor which medicine is more suitable. Inform your health professional of everything you take and ask them.
r.diet = I cannot indicate diets for diseases nor intake targets. Talk about the amount of food and your weight goals with your health professional. I can give general meal ideas to help with protein and hydration.
r.pregnancy = I cannot advise on medicines or food in pregnancy or in breastfeeding. Talk to your health professional.
r.minor = The AI records assistant is only for people over 18. If you have health doubts, talk to a health professional together with one of your parents or with a guardian.
r.other = I cannot help with that. I can always help organize your records, give general meal ideas or explain how to use the application.
