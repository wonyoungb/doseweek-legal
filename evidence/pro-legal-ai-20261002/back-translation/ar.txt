lead = It uses your records to create weekly summaries and general meal ideas, and explains the way to use the app. The answers are from artificial intelligence and not from a human, and it does not provide medical advice.
sent = Before sending the records we show you the content, and nothing is sent until you press "Send". We send the number of doses (on time, late, skipped), the change in the weight you entered yourself, the number of times each kind of symptom was recorded, the number of days on which a meal was recorded with the protein average and its goal, and the number of consecutive weeks of recording. When creating meal ideas we also send the meal type, the preferences, the allergies and the foods you do not like as you chose them. The language and region setting and the questions you write are also sent, and help in using the app adds only the app version, the platform and the current screen.
notSent = We do not send the names of medicines or their doses, nor the notes, nor the names of foods, nor the photos, nor the dates, nor your name or your e-mail or your account data. What you write in the question box is sent as you wrote it.
where = The app encrypts the content an additional time and sends it to the DoseWeek server (Seoul, Republic of Korea), and Amazon Bedrock from Amazon Web Services (Seoul region) processes it. The artificial-intelligence model is Claude from Anthropic. AWS operates it, and Anthropic cannot see the content.
retention = What you send and the answers do not remain on the server and are not used to train artificial intelligence. The answers are saved on your device only. Usage counters (without the content) are kept for a period of up to two months (2), and consent records as long as your account exists. If you report an answer and choose "Attach the text of the answer", that answer only is kept for 30 days.
e2ee = While the artificial intelligence creates the answer, the DoseWeek server and Amazon Bedrock can read what you sent. Sync and backup data remain protected by end-to-end encryption as they are now.
optional = Only the artificial-intelligence features will stop; the other Pro and Plus features remain available as they are. You can withdraw your consent at any time from Settings > AI records assistant.
