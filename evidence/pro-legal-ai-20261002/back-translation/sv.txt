lead = It uses your entries to create weekly summaries and general meal ideas and explains how the app works. The answers come from AI, not from a human, and it gives no medical advice.
sent = Before entries are sent we show the content, and nothing is sent until you press "Send". We send the number of doses (on time, late, skipped), the change in the weight you yourself entered, how many times you have logged each type of symptom, the number of days with a logged meal together with your protein average and protein goal, and your streak in weeks. For meal ideas we also send the meal type, the preferences, the allergies and the foods you do not like that you have chosen. Your language and region setting and the questions you write are also sent, and the app help adds only app version, platform and current screen.
notSent = We do not send medicine names or doses, notes, food names, photos, dates or your name, your e-mail address or your account details. What you write in the question field is sent exactly as you wrote it.
where = The app encrypts the content one more time and sends it to the DoseWeek server (Seoul, Republic of Korea), and Amazon Bedrock from Amazon Web Services (the Seoul region) processes it. The AI model is Claude from Anthropic. AWS runs it, and Anthropic cannot see the content.
retention = What you send and the answers are not saved on the server and are not used for AI training. The answers are saved only on your device. Usage counters (without content) are saved for up to 2 months and consent proofs as long as your account exists. If you report an answer and choose "Send along the answer text", only that answer is saved for 30 days.
e2ee = While the AI creates an answer, the DoseWeek server and Amazon Bedrock can read what you have sent. Sync and backup data are still protected with end-to-end encryption, just as now.
optional = It is only the AI functions that cannot be used; the other functions in Pro and Plus work as usual. You can withdraw the consent at any time under Settings > the AI log assistant.
