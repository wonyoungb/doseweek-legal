lead = Using your records it creates weekly summaries and general meal ideas and explains how the app is used. The answers are given not by a human but by artificial intelligence, and it does not give medical advice.
sent = Before the records are sent we show you the content, and nothing is sent until you tap "Send". Dose counts (on time, late, skipped), the change in the weight you entered yourself, how many times you recorded each symptom type, the number of days with a meal record together with your protein average and goal, and your streak in weeks are sent. For meal ideas the meal type you chose, preferences, allergies and foods you do not like are also sent. Your language and region setting and the questions you write are also sent; to app help only the app version, platform and current screen are added.
notSent = Medicine names or doses, notes, food names, photos, dates, or your name, e-mail address or account information are not sent. What you write in the question field is sent in the form you wrote it.
where = The app encrypts the content once more and sends it to the DoseWeek server (Seoul, Republic of Korea), and the Amazon Bedrock service of Amazon Web Services (Seoul region) processes the content. The artificial-intelligence model is Anthropic's Claude model. AWS operates the model and Anthropic cannot see the content.
retention = What you send and the answers are not kept on the server and are not used for artificial-intelligence training. The answers are saved only on your device. Usage counts (without content) are stored for at most 2 months, and consent records as long as your account exists. If you report an answer and choose the option "Also send the answer text", only that answer is stored for 30 days.
e2ee = While the artificial intelligence creates an answer, the DoseWeek server and Amazon Bedrock can read what you sent. Sync and backup data continue to be protected by end-to-end encryption as now.
optional = Only the artificial-intelligence features cannot be used; the other features of Pro and Plus continue to work. You can withdraw your consent whenever you want from Settings > AI record assistant.
checkHealth = [Optional] I accept the processing of my health (sensitive) information
checkDetail = Purpose: creating AI weekly summaries and meal ideas, answering questions about app use / Data: the records in the "What is sent" section above and the questions I write / Storage: deleted as soon as the answer is created (if an answer is attached to a report, only that answer 30 days) / If you refuse, everything other than artificial intelligence continues to work
checkAge = I am 18 years old or older
processing = The app encrypts the content, Amazon Bedrock (Seoul) processes it and the content is deleted as soon as the answer is created.
